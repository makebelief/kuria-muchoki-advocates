Frequently asked

Good questions are a starting point.

Contact the firm for guidance specific to your circumstances.

01

What happens after I make an enquiry?

The firm will review the outline, check whether it can assist and contact you about consultation arrangements and any documents required.

02

Does sending an enquiry make me a client?

No. An advocate-client relationship begins only after the firm confirms it can act and both sides agree on the engagement.

03

What should I bring to a consultation?

Bring identification and the documents directly relevant to the issue. Avoid sending confidential originals before the firm confirms the appropriate channel.
