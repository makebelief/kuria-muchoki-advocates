What we do

Ten areas. One standard of careful counsel.

Contact the firm if you are unsure which category your matter falls under. The first conversation can help identify the appropriate route.

01

Probate & Administration

Guidance through applications for grants, administration of estates, confirmation processes and the transfer of estate assets to beneficiaries.

02

Civil & Criminal Litigation

Case assessment, preparation and representation in civil disputes and criminal matters, with clear communication throughout the court process.

03

Land & Property Conveyancing

Support for property purchases, sales, transfers, leases, due diligence, documentation and the resolution of land-related questions.

04

Debt Collection

Structured advice on lawful recovery, including demand, negotiation, settlement, court proceedings and enforcement where appropriate.

05

Personal Injury & Material Damage

Advice and representation for claims arising from accidents, bodily injury, damaged property and related compensation questions.

06

Construction Law

Legal support for owners, contractors and other project participants on construction contracts, payments, obligations and disputes.

07

Family Law

Discreet and practical counsel through family-related matters, with attention to both the legal position and the sensitive personal context.

08

Consultancy

Focused legal opinions, document reviews and risk-based guidance to help individuals and organisations make informed decisions.

09

Corporate Law

Advice on business formation, governance, compliance, commercial relationships, transactions and ongoing corporate legal requirements.

10

Contracts

Drafting, review and negotiation of agreements, together with clear guidance on rights, obligations, performance and breach.

The descriptions above provide general information only. The appropriate legal approach depends on the facts and documents in each matter.
