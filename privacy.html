Privacy notice

Designed for discretion.

The enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. This website does not receive or store what you type into the form. If you send the email, it will be handled through your email provider and the firm email provider.

Do not send confidential documents, passwords, identification numbers or detailed case information until the firm confirms that it can act and provides an appropriate communication channel.

Sending an enquiry does not create an advocate-client relationship. The firm must first complete any required checks and confirm the terms of engagement.
