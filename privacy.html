Privacy notice

Designed for discretion.

When you use the enquiry form, the information you provide — such as your name, email address, telephone number, preferred contact method, practice area and message — is transmitted securely to the firm so that the firm can respond to your enquiry.

The website does not use advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. Form delivery may use a specialist email-delivery provider acting as a service provider. Technical platform logs may be retained for security, reliability and abuse prevention in accordance with the providers’ applicable retention practices.

Do not send confidential documents, passwords, identification numbers or detailed case information until the firm confirms that it can act and provides an appropriate communication channel.

Sending an enquiry does not create an advocate-client relationship. The firm must first complete any required checks and confirm the terms of engagement.

You may contact the firm at michaelmuchoki7@gmail.com about access, correction or deletion of personal information held through website correspondence, subject to applicable law and professional obligations.
